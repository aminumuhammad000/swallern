# Swallern Legal & Trust Readiness

This is a product checklist/template, not legal advice. Before public launch, have applicable counsel review
the final documents for the jurisdictions and services Swallern operates in.

Required documents:
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service
- Cookie/analytics notice where applicable
- Copyright/DMCA or equivalent takedown process where applicable
- Content/source attribution policy
- AI-assisted content disclosure where appropriate
- Account deletion/data handling process

Operational controls:
- Keep secrets server-side.
- Record consent/notice requirements where applicable.
- Provide a way to contact Swallern about copyright or factual corrections.
- Define retention/deletion behavior.
- Avoid collecting personal information that the product does not need.
