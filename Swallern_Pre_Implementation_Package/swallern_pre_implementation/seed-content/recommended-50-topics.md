## Selection logic
- Mix evergreen questions with a few topics that can later connect to trends.
- Prefer questions with clear explanations, strong source availability, and natural related-topic graphs.
- Cover the major Swallern categories without making the seed library feel like a school curriculum.
- Use these as development fixtures first; source verification and editorial review happen before publication.
