# Swallern Content Quality Standard v1

## Publishing principle
Swallern uses AI as a production and research assistant, never as an authority. Published educational claims must be traceable to appropriate sources.

## Source hierarchy
Use the strongest source type appropriate to the claim:
1. Primary research, official records, standards, government/educational institutions.
2. Peer-reviewed scholarship and established academic references.
3. Reputable reference works and specialist organizations.
4. High-quality journalism for current events/context.
5. Wikipedia and YouTube for discovery/reference, not automatic authority.

## Required checks
- No fabricated citations.
- Every material factual claim is source-traceable.
- Conflicting evidence is acknowledged when material.
- Uncertainty is represented.
- Time-sensitive claims include a date/context.
- Quotes are verified against their source.
- No plagiarism or near-copying.
- Topic is understandable to the intended audience.
- Related topics are genuinely relevant.

## Human review required
Mandatory review for sensitive/high-risk topics, medical/legal/financial advice, controversial current events,
new scientific claims, high-traffic topics, claims with significant source disagreement, and any content flagged by automated checks.

## AI generation rules
AI may draft explanations, questions, lessons, summaries, scripts, tags, and related topics.
AI must not invent sources, citations, statistics, quotations, study results, or consensus.

## Publishing gate
DISCOVERED -> RESEARCHING -> DRAFT -> REVIEW -> APPROVED -> PUBLISHED -> UPDATED
Rejected material may return to RESEARCHING or be archived.
